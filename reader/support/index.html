Reader Support

Support for Reader, Reader Dev, privacy questions, and data requests.

Contact

Email privacy@adz-dev-coding.pl for Reader support, privacy questions, or data requests.

What to include

  • app name: Reader or Reader Dev;
  • package identity if relevant: com.adzdevbox.mdredv2android or com.adzdevbox.mdredv2android.dev;
  • app version if available;
  • Android version and device model;
  • a short description of what happened and what you expected.

What not to include

Please do not include tokens, private documents, provider payloads, sensitive workspace names, or private repository content in support messages unless specifically requested for a narrow private diagnosis.

Basic troubleshooting

  • Confirm whether the issue happens in Reader, Reader Dev, or both.
  • Check that the file or folder is still available through Android's document picker.
  • For Google Drive or GitHub connector issues, check network access and reconnect the account or token if needed.
  • Try exporting a copy before clearing app data or reinstalling.
  • When reporting a repeatable issue, include the shortest set of steps that reproduces it.

Privacy Policy

The Reader Privacy Policy is available at https://adz-dev-coding.pl/reader/privacy/.
