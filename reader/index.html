Android app

Reader

A focused Android workspace for reading, writing, import, export, and local document workflows.

What Reader is for

Reader is designed for ordinary Android document tasks: creating, opening, editing, saving, importing, and exporting documents on your device.

Local document use does not require a Reader-specific account. Optional connector features, such as Google Drive or GitHub workflows, are used only when you choose them.

Stable identity

Reader uses package identity com.adzdevbox.mdredv2android.

Development identity

Reader Dev uses package identity com.adzdevbox.mdredv2android.dev for separate testing builds.

Privacy and support

The Reader Privacy Policy describes local storage, optional Google Drive and GitHub connector behavior, diagnostics, retention, deletion, and support contact details.

For help, include the app name, app version if available, Android version, and a short description of the issue.
